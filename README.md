# Administrative Operations & Document Management

## Project Overview

A portfolio case study demonstrating my experience supporting administrative operations through document organization, access management, communication group coordination, information maintenance, and administrative resource creation.

I support internal operations by helping keep information organized, accessible to the appropriate team members, and maintained across shared administrative resources.

## My Role

My responsibilities include:

- Adding volunteers to relevant department communication groups
- Adding volunteers to general volunteer and administrative communication groups
- Granting access to appropriate department folders in Google Drive
- Organizing scattered documents and moving files into their appropriate folders
- Identifying and correcting misplaced documents
- Maintaining department and project folders
- Updating organizational onboarding materials when project information changes
- Maintaining volunteer information and records
- Creating recruitment forms, assessment forms, and surveys
- Keeping administrative resources accessible to the appropriate team members
- Supporting appropriate access restrictions for sensitive information

## Administrative Workflow

The process generally involves:

1. Volunteer setup and communication group access
2. Department Google Drive access
3. Shared document organization
4. Document and project information maintenance
5. Volunteer information management
6. Administrative form creation
7. Access and information control

## Document Management

I help maintain an organized shared-drive environment by reviewing files, identifying documents stored in the wrong location, and moving them into the appropriate department or project folders.

## Access Management

Access is provided according to a person's role and responsibilities. Team members receive access to the resources they need, while sensitive volunteer information is restricted to authorized personnel.
